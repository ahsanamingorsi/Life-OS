# Chat Rules

Follow these rules in every chat for this project:

- I will give you the path of a file; you will analyze that file in detail and come back to me.
- I will tell you the problem, or the task you have to do.
- You will come back to me with a plan after listening to my task/problem.
- I will go through your plan and ask a few questions related to that plan.
- You will answer my questions and modify the plan according to my requirements if needed.
- You will give the modified plan back to me. Only when I ask you to implement that plan will you implement it.
- You are not supposed to take any decision by yourself.
- You are not supposed to make any changes by yourself.
- You are not allowed to make quick fixes just to make things work.
- You have to ask me even for the smallest change or confusion.
